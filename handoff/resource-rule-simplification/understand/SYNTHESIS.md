## 3. Cross-subsystem design decisions the spec must make

Each row: decision — recommended option — reason (maps that raised it). "Proposal table" rows are the proposal's own 设计决策点; the rest were raised by the maps. The recommended option is the one assumed by §1 and §4.

### Proposal decision table
- D-1 地图是否全连通 — 全连通 (random spanning tree + `n // 2` extra edges; loader rejects disconnected graphs, self-loops, duplicates, non-positive lengths, < 2 nodes). "每个节点至少一条边" does not imply connectivity (engine/industry evidence); delivery everywhere needs it. (map, categories, dataset)
- D-2 同类店铺的食品绑定 — explicit `store_ids | "all"` with `FOOD_BINDING_KIND_INCONSISTENT` rejection. The proposal's acceptance table pins "只给同类型的一家绑定食品必须拒绝"; by-kind binding would make that counter-example unexpressible. The dataset side prefers by-kind for authoring; it can derive explicit ids in its builder. (categories, storefront, dataset)
- D-3 session 的含义 — reuse the stay/transit `SessionState`; pushes are stored on the session (`SessionState.app_pushes`), so a session switch clears eligibility without a hook and the checkpoint seals it. Arrival-second pushes belong to the new stay session (the effect runs after `_switch_session_on_arrival`). (clock, content, footprints, actions, as2)
- D-4 推送频率 — hourly compiled ticks 08:00..21:00 local per person (`start < due < end`, `fold=0`), ≤ 1 per app per session, uniform over eligible apps then uniform over contents; no catch-up. Deterministic via `pending_events` + `random_state`; ~14 events per person per day for an 84-day run is acceptable (`pending_events` is a sorted list; alternative rolling tick re-armed by the handler is a one-line change later). (clock, scenario, content)
- D-5 外卖时间 — fixed prep + graph distance ÷ fixed courier speed (`DELIVERY_PREP_SECONDS = 900`, `DELIVERY_SPEED_MPS = 6.0`); fee `DELIVERY_FEE_FEN = 500` added once per order; distance never limits eligibility. Evidence in `DeliveryState.path/distance_meters/prep_seconds`. (map, storefront, footprints)
- D-6 营业与供给 — no stock, stores serve all day: delete `opening_hours.py`, calendars, exceptions, `MERCHANT_*`, `LOCATION_HOURS_CHANGE` source, supply events. Fewest states; the alternative (per-kind fixed hours) would keep `opening_hours.py` on a WorldMock table and the closing-warning wake reason. (storefront, clock, observe, dataset)
- D-7 做饭是否保留 — delete cooking, `inventory_recipe.py`, way 制作, `recipe_output_bindings`, ingredient data, the 料理 ledger rows; no fixed-duration cooking lifestyle item is added (datasets may declare one in `lifestyle/items.json` without engine support). (food, storefront, actions, tests)
- D-8 食品常数 — adopt 200/100/600 kcal and 5/3/20 min per portion; keep `portion_duration` (`max(60, round(full × portion))`), the 60–14400 s consume window, `sleep.food_utilization_efficiency` and the energy cap (proposal: 固定热量不能默默取消利用效率和上限). (food, categories)
- D-9 内容进度与重看 — shared progress per `content_id` across apps, re-watch allowed (`completed_rounds`), no exclusion set, no exhausted-pool stop rule. (content, footprints)
- D-10 工作工时 — actual seconds (`elapsed_seconds` of non-failed WORK activities on the topic); delete `effective_work_seconds`, efficiency factors, notes multiplier, `activity_efficiency`, `BodySettlement.work_efficiency`, `sleep.work_efficiency`. Fatigue stays as energy drain by MET. (work, actions, footprints, as2)

### Raised by the maps
- D-11 Ordering while in transit — no new request field; self-delivery orders while in transit are refused (`ACTIVITY_PRECONDITION_UNMET`, `unmet=delivery_destination`, "Place the order before departing or after arriving."); orders for others go to the recipient's receive location as today. Honours "路途中下单必须明确一个地点" without changing `start_activity`'s schema or the frozen "收货地点不作参数" rule. (map, storefront, actions)

(actions, as2, observe, footprints, content)

1. Ledger lock-in: ≈ 30 of the 147 byte-frozen mutations, ≈ 160 of 331 owner requirements and five archived-feature ledgers pin behaviour the proposal deletes; every partial edit is a red build, so the branch cannot be gate-green until stage 3 and the 147 guard must be relaxed up front.
2. No usable environment: the new loader rejects both the in-repo fixture and the dataset's `zhangjiang-baseline`; a rich artificial fixture (same-kind stores with/without apps, far node, apps with content, a work topic) must be hand-authored in stage 1 or nothing runs, and the dataset must re-publish before the documented full tier is meaningful.
3. Complete-day evidence: the scripted two-person day and `viewer-context.json` are Zhangjiang- and old-protocol-specific; they must be rewritten on the fixture and re-frozen on POSIX (third baseline) while the two historical baselines keep their bytes.
