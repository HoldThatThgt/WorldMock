# Subsystem map: entertainment content, catalogs, watch/listen activities, games, lifestyle fillers

Worktree read: `D:/code/WorldMock/.claude/worktrees/rrs` (read-only). Dataset checkout consulted read-only at `D:/code/WorldMock-Dataset` (HEAD `2cff671`, not the `7417b63` quoted in dataset-evidence.md; the content counts below match that newer HEAD).
Proposal: `docs/dev/PROPOSAL_2026-10-06_RESOURCE_AND_RULE_SIMPLIFICATION.md` (娱乐 / app / 推送 sections). All paths below are repo-relative to the worktree unless absolute.

Summary of the current shape in one paragraph: the person picks a **content item by title** (`watch` ways 接着看/从头再看/接着听/从头再听) or a **TextWorld game by title** (`play` way 游玩) or a **lifestyle item** (`do` way 进行). Content is a scenario `contents[]` entry (8 kinds, SRT timeline + hash, optional MP3 for podcasts, per-item `entertainment_relief_per_hour`) whose subtitle cues are revealed by real media position; progress lives in `AgentState.content_progress`. Games are scenario `games[]` entries validated against TextWorld JSON files (hash + walkthrough hash) but no longer have a command loop (play is a `watch`-shaped activity with a `game_feedback` boundary). Lifestyle items come from `corpora/lifestyle/items.json` with per-item `effects`. There is **no app concept at runtime** for entertainment: apps exist only as purchase channels derived from the optional phone config (`profile/coverage_apps.py`) and as dataset-side after-the-fact projections (`tools/simulate_phone.py: content_apps_by_id / game_apps_by_id`). There is **no push notification**, no app session, no per-session pinned content.

## 8. Open questions the spec must decide

1. **Content kinds enum names and occupancy.** Proposal fixes 文章/图文/短视频/长视频/实况 but not internal ids nor resource claims; current table says podcast = ears only, drivers may listen. Decide: do 实况/长视频 claim `eyes`+`ears` and is any kind audio-only (driving + app use)? If none, `kinds.watch` loses conditional claims entirely.
2. **Relief constants per kind** (proposal: "娱乐效果…由 WorldMock 固定") — values per kind, and whether `exposure_count` dilution and `entertainment_relief_preference_floor` survive once `taste_weights` are gone (决策点 「内容进度与重看」: "跨 app 共享进度、允许重看" recommended; the alternative "看完后排除" needs a stop rule for exhausted pools).
3. **Activity duration semantics**: "人物只选 app 和使用时长" — is the duration free-form (bounded 60–86400) or derived? Current `derived_activity_duration` derives from content; `SPEC-agent-interaction` says "模型不填时长". Decide whether app use gets a fixed default (e.g., feedback interval) with 30-min reports, and what happens to `pause`/`resume` of an app activity whose current content completes while paused.
4. **Games**: proposal table "TextWorld 独立游戏链…整体退出" — confirm `play_game` kind, `games[]`, TextWorld dependency and Linux x86_64 gate are deleted (not just the content kind), and whether any "游戏" survives as an app-bound content kind (it is not among the five).
5. **Push mechanics** (决策点 「session 的含义」, 「推送频率」): reuse stay/transit sessions (recommended); hourly cadence 08:00–21:00 per person, one per app per session, uniform over eligible apps; confirm the push is delivered during sleep like messages (deferred to first boundary after waking) or skipped; confirm no wake-up is forced ("遵守当前睡眠和活动中断约束") — i.e., push arrives as a progress-layer record without forcing a decision boundary, or does it create one?
6. **Pinned-content lifecycle edge**: content completed in another app clears the push ("任一绑定 app 完成"); a content consumed *before* the push and not completed — push still pins it and resumes from position (proposal says yes). Decide whether a push may pin content the person already completed (random draw may pick it; proposal allows re-watch).
7. **App catalog ownership and location**: single `apps` list in the scenario vs a catalog file under `catalog/`; must be the same identity used by store bindings (storefront subsystem) and by phone export; name uniqueness rules (same name as a location?), and whether apps with neither stores nor contents are rejected at registration.
8. **Summary provenance fields**: "真实摘要保留来源信息，合成摘要明确标记合成；时长标记为仿真数值" — exact field names (`summary_source: real|synthetic`, `source_url`, `consumption_seconds_source: synthetic`) and whether `CorpusManifest` still binds anything for content (no files) or content lives entirely in the scenario/catalog JSON.
9. **Lifestyle fillers vs apps**: proposal removes "「刷手机」却不记录 app 的重复活动入口"; decide whether WorldMock rejects lifestyle items whose name/category looks like app use (cannot be done robustly) or simply requires the dataset to delete them, and whether `families.lifestyle` `content_kinds` (9 categories) stay as the fixed classification ("分类规则在 WorldMock 钉死" → yes, enumerate them in code, dataset cannot add).
10. **Footprint/phone export shape**: `played` rows per content switch inside one app activity (multiple contents per activity) and the new `app_id` on activity snapshots; whether `content_push` events are exported to LabelKit as notifications.
11. **Format rejection list**: enumerate old keys that must be rejected explicitly with English errors (`timeline_path`, `audio_path`, `entertainment_relief_per_hour`, `experience_label`, `games`, `phone_simulation_config` for apps, `taste`-related persona fields `content_genres`?) versus silently absent — proposal says "旧规则字段直接报错".
12. **Candidate/presentation contract for apps**: are apps subject to rotation/`candidate_presentation_limit` at all (few objects) or always fully listed; does `callable_index` keep a `contents` key (renamed `apps`) → affects `prompt_sha256` re-pin and `tests/integration/test_seven_layer_request.py`.
