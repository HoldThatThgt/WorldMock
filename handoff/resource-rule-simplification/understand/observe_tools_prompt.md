## 8. Open questions for the spec

1. **How does the person say "use an app for N minutes"?** `start_activity.items[]` has no duration field; today every duration is derived (content remaining, feedback interval). Options: derive `app_use` duration = `activity_feedback_seconds` (model ends/continues at each feedback, no schema change) vs add `duration` to the request (changes `tool_schema_sha256`, new error paths). Proposal §娱乐 says "人物只选 app 和使用时长" — the spec must pick one; the derived option is the smallest.
2. **Which app pays/delivers when a store binds several apps and `from` is null?** `prefer` only orders stores. Options: require `from=<app>` when the store has >1 app (new `SOURCE_REQUIRED` rejection), or deterministic tie-break (first app by id) written to `details.notes`. Proposal decision table does not cover it.
3. **Should apps be candidates (rotation/cap) or always-listed targets?** §5.3 recommends always listed (catalog is small, real names). If the spec wants a cap, `CandidateKind` must add `app` and `callable_index.apps` must exist for by-name calling.
4. **Content progress visibility.** Proposal: progress shared across apps, resume from position, re-watch allowed (decision 内容进度与重看). Where does the model see "《A》看了一部分"? Options: app target `state` (one line) vs only in results/settled sentences. Also whether `exposure_count` stays in state.
5. **Work: keep a deadline/notification chain?** Proposal keeps only topic + target hours and reuses scenario work place/shifts; it is silent on `due_at`, `task_deadline` records, `work_available` notification mails (`related_task_id`), `visible_task_ids` disclosure and `paid_task_ids`. Smallest design: topics are assigned at scenario level and visible from run start; no deadline; "complete" = `done ≥ target` and the topic leaves the tool layer; wages untouched. The spec must say so explicitly (decision 工作工时 covers only actual vs effective seconds).
6. **Browse of the product catalog.** Not mentioned by the proposal. With same-type menus and `query_world`, `浏览`/`BROWSE_TARGETS`/supply visibility TTL/pinned browse results are redundant; deleting them removes `catalog` target kind, `browse_items`, D17. Keep or delete must be decided (keeping means visibility TTL must be re-specified without `delivery_destination_ids`).
7. **Opening hours and stock** (decision 营业与供给): if hours are kept, `ELIGIBILITY_CLOSED_HOURS`/`MERCHANT_CLOSED` and `place_row.opening_status` stay; if stock is dropped, `ELIGIBILITY_OUT_OF_STOCK`, `OUT_OF_STOCK`, `PoolEntry.stock`, `supply_stock` go. The tool-layer `state` wording ("开着/关着/开放时间不明") depends on it.
8. **Cooking** (decision 做饭是否保留): decides whether 「制作」way, recipe bindings and `CATEGORY_PAIRING_*`/`INVENTORY_RECIPE_*` reasons survive in the tool layer and vocabulary.
9. **Persona tastes vs contents without genre.** `tastes.content_genres` and `taste_weights` currently drive content ordering; with random draws per app they only affect products/lifestyle. Decide whether contents keep an optional `genre` for taste (proposal: "不需要推荐画像") and what `persona_coverage` checks (`media` vs app catalog names).
10. **Push wording and capacity.** Whether the push record carries the full summary (proposal: "摘要可以在推送和打开时完整展示") — long summaries × up to 14 pushes/day inflate the progress layer; the spec may cap summary length at registration or fold pushes first in `prompt_capacity`.
11. **Resource claims for `app_use`.** Since 2026-10-06 claims are kind names and only the same kind excludes itself (`spec-parallel-activities-占用声明`). Proposal: one entertainment activity at a time; driving + podcast parallelism disappears with the podcast kind. The spec must (a) give `app_use` its own claim name and delete `watch`, `screen`, `play_game` (closed set 11 → 9) or keep `watch` for it; (b) add `app_use` to driving's conditional list (no audio exception) or keep an exception for `实况`/audio-like content kinds — which would need a `content_kind:*` conditional and a surviving `screen`-like name; (c) decide whether 吃 ∥ app_use and 生活活动 ∥ app_use stay allowed (today 吃 ∥ 观看 and 生活活动 ∥ 观看 are allowed by 乙).
